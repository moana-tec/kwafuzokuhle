ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

A Foundation for Lifelong Success

Kwa-Fuzokuhle Primary School is deeply rooted in the community, providing an inclusive, disciplined, and stimulating environment where every child can flourish academically, socially, and creatively.

Our Vision

To produce honest, loyal, just responsible future leaders, learners who are critical thinkers so that they become independent competent & marketable through consistent and fair education.

Our Mission

The school will work in collaboration with the community so that each and every learner gets quality education.

CORE VALUES & PILLARS

What Guides Us Everyday

  • A
    Educating our learners in totality: Fostering holistic growth across academic, social, and emotional dimensions.
  • B
    Dedication, commitment and teamwork: Working hand in hand with staff and learners to reach common goals.
  • C
    Parental involvement in learners progress: Partnering closely with families to support every child's educational journey.
ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

Curriculum & Learning Phases

Foundation Phase

Grades R – 3

Focuses on basic literacy, numeracy, and foundational life skills in a nurturing environment.

Home Language (isiZulu) First Additional Language (English) Mathematics Life Skills

Intermediate Phase

Grades 4 – 6

Broadens the curriculum, introducing critical thinking, scientific inquiry, and social sciences.

isiZulu HL English FAL Mathematics Natural Sciences & Technology Social Sciences Life Skills

Senior Phase

Grade 7

Prepares learners for the transition to high school with specialized subject instruction and leadership opportunities.

isiZulu HL English FAL Mathematics Natural Sciences Social Sciences Technology Economic & Management Sciences Life Orientation
